PAM Workflows

PAM workflows are COMPLEX, and while many components of these workflows are efficient or highly automated, many workflows contain points of high friction. As we transition from a PAM Strategic Initiative to operational National PAM Program, we need to understand and streamline the complete PAM workflows from data collection to management products. We also need to identify and prioritize alleviating points of friction along our high value workflows.

In preparation for our PAM-SI meeting (February 1-5, 2027), we will need ALL science center PAM teams to develop workflows and metadata for their high priority workflows.

Improving High Value Workflows

We have developed user-friendly tools to minimize the effort required to develop your PAM Workflows (and necessary metadata) and to maximize the utility of this effort for your own needs.

The initial goals of the PAM Workflow are to:

  • Create a visual representation for each of your high value workflows (Flowchart)

  • Evaluate current state for each workflow component

  • Identify barriers and needs for improvement

  • We encourage you to create these for ALL your workflows, but the PAM-SI will need these for high-value workflows.

  • We will provide training & help during the PAM Community Co-working sessions.

Next Steps:

  • Cross-Center Workflow Teams: teams with similar workflows will meet prior to the PAM-SI meeting to synthesize their results, identify points of friction, and provide information to support solutions to address these frictions points.

  • Adaptation of workflows for personal use. We will provide instructions/training for teams to adapt these workflows to meet their needs.

  • PAM-SI meeting: We will spend a day discussing these current state of workflows and how they can be improved.
